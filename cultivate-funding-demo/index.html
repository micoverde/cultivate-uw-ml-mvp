The Challenge

Why questioning techniques matter in early childhood education

❓

Open vs Closed Questions

Research shows open-ended questions (OEQ) promote deeper thinking and language development, yet educators often default to closed-ended questions (CEQ) that limit responses to yes/no.

OEQ "What do you notice about the flower?"
CEQ "Is the flower red?"
⏱️

Wait Time Matters

Studies show children need 3-5 seconds to formulate thoughtful responses. Many educators move on too quickly, missing opportunities for deeper engagement.

1s Insufficient
3-5s Optimal
📊

Feedback Gap

Educators rarely receive objective feedback on their questioning patterns. Traditional coaching is expensive and infrequent, limiting professional growth.

2x/year Typical coaching visits
Real-time Our AI feedback
