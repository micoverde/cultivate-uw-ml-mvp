📝 Expert Annotation Process

Trained researchers review each video segment, identifying educator questions and annotating them with precise timestamps, question types (OEQ/CEQ), and contextual descriptions including wait time behavior.

Question Type Definitions

OEQ Open-Ended Question

Questions that encourage extended responses, critical thinking, and elaboration. Examples: "What do you think...?", "How can we...?", "Why did that happen?"

CEQ Closed-Ended Question

Questions with limited response options (yes/no, specific answers). Examples: "Is this red?", "How many are there?", "Do you want more?"

📊 Training Data Schema

After annotation, audio is transcribed using Whisper AI and processed through our feature extraction pipeline, generating 56 linguistic and interaction features for each training sample.

ML Training Features (56 total)

Feature Category Features Description
Linguistic word_count, character_count, avg_word_length, sentence_count Basic text statistics
Question Analysis question_count, has_question, question_type_score Question presence and type indicators
Complexity text_complexity_score, avg_sentence_length, educator_language_score Linguistic sophistication metrics
Interaction provides_wait_time, creates_response_opportunity, overall_interaction_quality Pedagogical interaction patterns
Confidence transcription_confidence, avg_word_confidence Whisper transcription reliability

🔒 Privacy & IRB Compliance

All data collection and processing follows strict privacy protocols and IRB-approved procedures to protect the identities of children and educators in the videos.

🛡 Data Protection Measures

  • All video data stored in secure, access-controlled environments
  • Child and educator names replaced with pseudonyms in transcripts
  • No personally identifiable information (PII) in training datasets
  • IRB approval obtained for all research activities
  • Data retention policies follow University of Washington guidelines
  • Audio transcripts de-identified before ML processing
  • Original video files never included in public repositories
